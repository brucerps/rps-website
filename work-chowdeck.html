Case study · Chowdeck
What we did
Video editingReelsCaptions

Chowdeck is a food delivery app from Lagos with a loud, funny voice online. Its videos move fast and speak the way its customers speak.

We edited short videos for the brand's social channels: skits, vendor and rider stories, and food clips. The work was in the timing. We cut to the punchline, added captions in the brand's tone, picked sound that fits the joke, and kept each video short enough to be watched twice. Every video was delivered in upright format, ready to post.

  • Editing of skits, stories and food clips
  • Captions in the brand's tone
  • Sound and music
  • Upright versions ready to post
