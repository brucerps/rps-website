services: Social media content, Reels, Product stories
by: Bruce N., our founder, as a freelancer before RPS

Ere Perez is an Australian natural cosmetics brand. Its buyers want to know what is in a product and how to use it, and they want to see it on real skin.

We produced social content built on those questions. Texture shots showed what a product looks like. Short how-to reels showed it going on. Ingredient posts explained what is inside and why. We planned the content in sets for each product, edited it in soft natural colour to suit the brand, and delivered it with captions, ready to post.

- Content sets for each product
- How-to reels and texture shots
- Ingredient explainers
- Captions and post order
