services: Website, Booking pages, Mobile

Intercape runs intercity coaches across South Africa and neighbouring countries. Most of its customers book on a phone, often on a slow connection, so the website has to be light and plain.

We worked on the pages a traveller meets on the way to a ticket: routes, timetables and travel information. We put the details people look for first, such as departure points, times and luggage rules, and shortened the steps between choosing a route and booking. Pages were tested on small screens and slow connections, and written in plain words.

- Route and timetable pages
- Fewer steps from route to booking
- Travel information in plain words
- Tested on phones and slow connections
